Process notes for a person:
- WebSearch ran out early in research, so press and status-news coverage is thin for most venues. Many profiles rest on the venue's own pages, council pages or listing sites (ma.to, DesignMyNight, Skiddle) and are therefore short. Aggregator-only claims are attributed to the listing site in the text.
- One reviews researcher ran a single `curl` against glovethatfits.co.uk/robots.txt from Bash (502, no content returned). Nothing from it was used. Worth a word with the routine owner, since agents should only read pages with `page_text.py`.
- `check_quote.py` cannot read the nested findings format, so some quotes were copied from `page_text.py` output and never re-fetched. The checker's re-fetch is the first real test for those.
