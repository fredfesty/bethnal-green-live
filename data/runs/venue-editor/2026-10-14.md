# Venue editor run, 2026-10-14

Worked from the files only; `scripts/zone.py` and `venuelib.match_known` were the only scripts run for new venues. Evidence is copied exactly from leads and findings. `validate.py venues data/venues` passes (116/116). New and changed profiles have no `review` newer than `profiled_at`, so they stay hidden until the checker has been through them.

## New venues added (4)

- **Hare** (core, 731 m; found by map): independent free house at 505 Cambridge Heath Road. CAMRA is the only source. It lists live music, but no event or kind of music was found, so whether that is current is unconfirmed (the profile says so). No accessible toilet; one shallow step at the entrance, so `step_free` is left null.
- **Hoxton Hound** (core, 1,534 m; found by map): pub in railway arches by Hoxton Station. Weekly Wednesday quiz from the operator's page; CAMRA gives step-free access and an accessible toilet (listing last updated 5 Aug 2026). No live-music night found.
- **Hoxton Cabin** (core, 1,567 m; found by listings): bar on Kingsland Road with comedy, vinyl open decks, karaoke and progressive/melodic house (Red Eye). Rests on ma.to (aggregator) and CAMRA; no own website. `step_free` left null because some nights are in a basement and no source says how it is reached.
- **The Lauriston** (fringe, 1,370 m; found by web): pub at 162 Victoria Park Road, E9 7JN (postcode from CAMRA). Programme from its own what's-on page: Wednesday quiz, Thursday Irish sessions, last-Saturday karaoke, Saturday live bands and DJs. The 2006-dated Time Out and 2014 Londonist pages were used only for the name and "pub", not for music.
