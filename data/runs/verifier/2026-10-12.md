## Scout feedback

- Age and genre quotes: on 93 Feet East and DICE the scout quotes bare tag lines ("18+ Indie Indie Rock", "This is an 18+ event") that fail the title-nearby test and were removed. Quote the tag together with the title, or use the description sentence. Example: 2026-10-16-93-feet-east-hotvox-presents-ammari-one-stop-away.
- Lineups: quotes like "Lineup X, Y" or "Support 8:00 pm Spanish Horses 9:00 pm" often have no title nearby on DICE. Example: 2026-10-22-sebright-arms-spanish-horses-support-in-london-son-estrella-galicia.
- Multi-line-up gigs: DICE title, lineup section and description can disagree (2026-11-05-sebright-arms-recount-...). Compare them and note it.
- ma.to (Star of Bethnal Green): the quote must include the venue name; a venue field backed only by the title/date line cannot be confirmed. Example: 2026-10-16-star-of-bethnal-green-jubel-dj-crooks-goons.
- Troxy: put the "Doors Open / Event Starts" time and the date in one quote with the title where possible, or the start time is unconfirmed (2026-10-18-troxy-sweetnotes).
- Pages that contradict themselves (93FE descriptions with another date or time, BGWMC "close 2.30AM" vs 2:00 AM): the scout flagged these in notes, which helped; for date or start-time conflicts consider leaving the candidate out.
- "From free" prices: the page supports a minimum of 0 only. Leave `is_free` unset or the price unknown rather than `is_free: false`.
- Hackney Empire: date-only candidate with no venue quote and no year. Skip pages whose times block is empty until the scout can read them.
